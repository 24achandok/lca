End-of-Life Strategy

  • Disassembly – Designed with screws and snap-fits to separate materials easily.
  • Plastics – PLA parts can be industrially composted; PETG sorted and recycled.
  • Metals – Brass and aluminium sent to scrap metal recycling (high recovery rate).
  • Wood – Ash and mahogany can be repurposed or composted if untreated.
  • PCBs & electronics – Collected via WEEE scheme; precious metals recovered.
  • Batteries – Return to retailer or battery recycling point.

Circular potential: Over 70% of the product by mass can be recycled or composted with proper sorting.

Design Improvements

  • Material substitution – Replace PETG with recycled PLA or bio-based PET.
  • Brass alternative – Use stainless steel or aluminium alloy with lower impact.
  • Wood sourcing – Specify FSC-certified Ash & Mahogany.
  • Modular electronics – Use socketed components for easier repair & upgrade.
  • Battery – Switch to rechargeable Li-ion pack (reduces waste over time).
  • 3D printing – Optimise infill & use 100% recycled filament.
  • Packaging – 100% recycled cardboard with no plastic inserts.

Net impact reduction: Estimated 25–35% lower carbon footprint with these changes.
